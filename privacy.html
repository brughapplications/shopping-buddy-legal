Shopping Buddy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Operator: Brugh Applications LLC (“we,” “us”)

Contact: brughapplications@gmail.com

App: Shopping Buddy

Terms: Terms of Service

1. Overview

This Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using Shopping Buddy you agree to this Policy.

2. Information we collect

Account: email address and user ID for sign-in and identification. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider; we do not receive or store your password in a form we can read or use.

Shopping & recipe content: list titles and items; recipes (titles, ingredients, servings, source type, source URL when you import a link); favorites; pantry/fridge items and related dates/notes; preferences (appearance, units).

Camera & photos: If you allow access, camera and photo library are used only to analyze recipe images and extract ingredient/recipe text to help build your lists. We do not run a permanent cloud photo gallery; we aim to keep extracted text/structured recipe data in your account rather than long-term photo storage. You can deny or revoke camera/photos in iOS Settings (extraction features that need them will not work).

External URLs: When you paste a recipe link, we fetch that page (or use a processing function) to extract ingredients for your list and may store the source URL with the recipe so you can open it again.

Usage / improvement data: We may collect app usage and content signals (for example how items are categorized, text patterns in lists/recipes) to improve categorization, text cleaning, and product experience. This is for operating and improving Shopping Buddy—not for selling your personal shopping profile.

Ads & purchase: If you use the free ad-supported version, ad partners may collect device/ad identifiers and interaction data as described in their policies and in App Store nutrition labels. If you buy Remove Ads ($3.99), Apple processes the payment; we receive purchase/entitlement status (not your full card number).

We do not collect: your password in usable form; contacts; precise GPS for “shopping route” (aisle organization is not location tracking); payment card numbers (Apple handles IAP).

3. How we use information

  • Provide sync, lists, pantry, favorites, and account features
  • Run recipe extraction from images and URLs you provide
  • Improve categorization, text cleaning, and overall UX
  • Show ads (unless you purchased Remove Ads) and restore that purchase
  • Secure the service, prevent abuse, and comply with law
  • Communicate important service notices

4. Sharing

Processors help us run the app (for example Supabase for authentication and database; ad networks for ads; Apple for IAP). They process data on our instructions or as independent controllers where required (ads/Apple).

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share your personal shopping lists or email with third parties for their own marketing. If that ever changes, we will update this Policy, update App Store privacy disclosures, and notify users as required before relying on the new practice.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

5. Retention

We keep account and app content while your account is active. When you delete items in-app, we remove them from active use. When you delete your account, we remove your account and associated personal data from active systems as soon as reasonably practicable (typically right away after you confirm deletion). Copies in backups or logs may persist for a short period and are then deleted or overwritten on the normal backup cycle (generally within 30 days), except where law or security requires limited longer retention.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Edit or delete lists/recipes/pantry in-app
  • Delete Account in Settings (or email us)
  • Turn off camera/photos in iOS Settings
  • Purchase Remove Ads to stop our in-app ad display (subject to Apple billing)
  • Email brughapplications@gmail.com for access/deletion requests
  • California/other US state rights (know, delete, opt out of “sale”/sharing): we do not sell personal information as described above

7. Children’s privacy

Not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us to delete if you believe we have.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, provider access controls). No method is 100% secure.

9. International transfers

Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where we or our processors operate.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change; material changes (including any future decision to sell or share personal data for advertising beyond current ads SDKs) will be disclosed in an updated Policy and App Store labels, with notice as required. Continued use after the effective date means acceptance, except where law requires additional consent.
